## Limitations & honest findings

- **In-sample optimism.** The headline in-sample Sharpe is consistently 1.5–2× the OOS number once cost assumptions match reality.
- **Cost sensitivity.** At 5 bps per trade (vs. the 2 bps default) the OOS Sharpe falls to roughly half. Borrow-rate stress on the short leg is not yet modelled.
- **Capacity.** Square-root impact implies non-trivial Sharpe degradation above ~$5M per leg for this pair pair; this is liquidity-floor stuff, not institutional scale.
- **Regime fragility.** KO/PEP cointegration weakens post-2022; the Kalman filter adapts, but signal frequency drops.
- **Single pair.** A real production deployment would diversify across 20–50 cointegrated pairs and re-screen monthly.

These are the questions a hiring manager *would* ask. Calling them out preemptively is part of the point of this repository.
